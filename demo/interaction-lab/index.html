Touch Lab

Every control on this page runs on Trompe's interaction layer: press travel, springs, drag physics, focus and keyboard handling. Try them with a mouse, a finger and the keyboard.

Buttons

Tap as fast as you can: the press still shows for 90 ms. Press, then drag off to cancel. Hold Space to hold it down.

Cards and rows

A card tips a fraction of a degree toward where your finger lands, and settles back on a spring.

Switches

Click, or grab the thumb and push it. It resists in its own half, then snaps over the centre and flips before you let go.

Checkboxes and radios

The box dips under the press, the tick draws itself and the radio dot springs in.
