      Frost, a calendar in edge-lit acrylic

      Every surface is a slab of acrylic: frosted for the month grid and date picker, clear for the header, side panel and editor. Each slab has a polished arris that catches the light on one side and a darker refracted edge on the other, an internal reflection band, frosting grain, and a soft cast shadow on the concrete with a caustic rim where the polished edge focuses light. Satin aluminium standoffs hold the slabs off the base; brushed LED rails clamp along the top edges and light the engraved grid lines from inside the glass. Today glows warm amber. Events are vitreous enamel chips in a silver cloison.

      Turn the light dial in the top bar: every edge, shadow, caustic, gloss and engraving moves with it. Nothing uses images; three surfaces use a backdrop blur (the editor, the listbox and the gallery bar).

      • Glass push keys with press travel, a latching Day / Week / Month group, enamel primary key.
      • Mini month grid: arrows, Home/End, PageUp/PageDown (Shift for years), Enter opens the day. A clear lens slides to the selected date and magnifies it.
      • Enamel bead checkboxes and radios, sandblasted text pockets, a custom listbox (arrows, Home/End, type-ahead, Esc), a glass slide switch with an amber inner light, a detented glass slider.
      • Drag an event chip to another day; with the keyboard, focus a chip and press Alt + arrows, or edit its date.
