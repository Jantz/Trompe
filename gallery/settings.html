Material world

Alloy is a settings app cut from one aluminium slab: a bead-blasted face, a diamond-cut chamfer that flashes where the light falls, and a smoked-glass pane with the chassis' milled honeycomb faintly visible beneath it. Settings sit on brushed plates screwed to the glass. Dark re-anodises the body graphite; the chrome hardware (fader cap, slide actuators, domes, screws, jewel bezels) stays bright metal.

Every face, bevel, chamfer and shadow reads --tr-light-angle, so the light dial re-lights the slab. LEDs and the LCD are emissive. No images: gradients, SVG noise tiles, inline icons.

Controls

  • Sidebar: milled pockets; the current section rises as a polished key with a blue LED. Search filters it.
  • Appearance: interlocking latching keys (← →) that re-theme the slab. Text size: domed keys around a seven-segment LCD.
  • Brightness: fader cap in a milled slot with a light-pipe fill; it dims the glass. Night shift: slide switch and amber LED. Schedule: glass listbox (Enter, arrows, type-ahead, Esc). HDR: disabled, with its reason.
  • Resolution: native radios as chrome-ringed jewels. Options: native checkboxes as latching keys with etched, backlit checks.
  • Display name validates (clear it). Restore defaults opens a <dialog>; Apply is the glossy anodised key.
