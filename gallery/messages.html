    Pebble

    An everyday messaging screen whose parts are moulded rather than drawn. The body and every control are soft-touch silicone: matte, with wide bevels that roll off slowly, a slightly lighter, warmer rim where light scatters inside the rubber, and a fine grain. Keys sit in moulded web grooves and squash wider as they travel. Outgoing messages, avatars and the send key are cast resin under a thick clear coat, so the light leaves a crisp window reflection on the lit edge, a saturated caustic glow on the far side and a translucent coloured shadow. Reactions and unread counts are enamel on brass; menus, the delete dialog and toasts are frosted polycarbonate with lit edges.

    Everything is CSS gradients, box-shadows, one tiled grain texture and a little SVG (icons and the procedural photos). Every shadow, highlight, caustic and seam reads the scene light, so the light dial in the top bar re-lights the whole product.

    • Search well (/ to focus, Esc to clear) and All / Unread / Groups latching keys
    • Conversation rows, header call / details / more keys, a more menu (arrows, Home/End, type-ahead, Esc) with mute, pin, archive (undo) and delete
    • Delete dialog with a silicone checkbox, secondary and destructive buttons
    • Per-message react button with an enamel reaction menu; pins spring in
    • Composer: attach menu, growing textarea, emoji palette, send key (Enter sends, Shift+Enter adds a line), retry on failed messages
    • Details pad: notifications slide switch and a stepped text-size slider
