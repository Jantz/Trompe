          Waymark field journal

          A trip planner made of soft goods: oxblood pebble-grain leather with waxed saddle stitching, a blind-debossed compass re-lit by the scene light, an elastic corner band, a flesh-side lining with a press-stud pocket, cream dot-grid paper and cut-card index tabs. No images: CSS gradients and procedural SVG only.

          Underneath are ordinary app controls (tablist, select-only combobox, checkboxes, text field and textarea, primary and secondary buttons, a card list and a modal dialog) that happen to be leather, card, ink and brass.

          Controls

          • Elastic band: drag it off the corner until it snaps, or focus it and press Enter, or use Open journal.
          • Index tabs: click, or arrow keys, Home, End. Each change turns a page.
          • Trip tag: Enter or ↓ opens, arrows move, type to jump, Esc closes.
          • Kit list ticks in pencil. Notes: New note writes an index card; × asks before tearing one out.
          • Press-stud opens the pocket. Stamp: press and release to ink it and stamp the open page (six per page max); tap the pad for red or indigo.
