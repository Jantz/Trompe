      Ember Home

      A smart-home screen built as if the interface were the wall itself: lime plaster with trowel relief that rakes with the light, flush bisque-ceramic plates set into shadow-gap recesses, and a dial thermostat in bead-blasted aluminium around a smoked, frosted-glass dome. The everyday app parts (room tabs, a scene menu, schedule toggles, a confirm dialog) are made from the same ceramic.

      Light comes from the scene light: the plaster relief is one height field differentiated four ways and mixed by the light angle, the window and leaf shadows slide across the wall, and every chamfer, gloss and cast shadow follows. The room temperature is simulated and drifts toward the set point while the page is visible. Lowering the shades dims the daylight, the fan sways the leaf shadows, and the dimmer warms the plaster.

      • Dial ring: drag around, flick to coast, arrow keys or PgUp/PgDn; 10 to 30 °C in half-degree clicks with a firmer detent on whole degrees.
      • Glass face: press (click, Enter, Space) to cycle Heat, Cool, Auto and Off.
      • Light dimmer: drag the ceramic cap or use arrow keys. Shades and Fan rockers: click, drag or Space.
      • Rooms: tabs with arrow keys; Scene: listbox with arrows, Enter, Esc and type-ahead; Schedule: native checkboxes; All off asks first.
