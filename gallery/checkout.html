      Kurogane

      An everyday sign-in and checkout for an invented shop of hand-made goods, built as if every control were made by the same lacquer workshop. The board is black urushi with a mirror gloss: the softbox you see reflected in it slides toward the light as you turn the light dial. Inputs are recessed wells lined with raw silk, labels are engraved and gilded, and the hardware (the show-password latch, quantity buttons, the gift-wrap slide) is brushed brass. The card preview is brushed shibuichi; it turns over when you reach the security code.

      No images: every surface is gradients, shadows and procedural SVG noise (silk slubs, brushing, nashiji gold dust). Shadows, rims, gloss and reflections all read the scene light.

      • Email and password wells with validation, a brass push-latch to show the password, a lacquer checkbox with a gold check inlay
      • Lacquer pebble buttons with press travel, plaque buttons, a text link, a remove (destructive) action
      • Knurled brass quantity buttons with detents and rolling numerals, shipping radio cards that sink when chosen
      • Country listbox: Enter/Space opens, arrows, Home/End, type-ahead, Esc closes
      • Gift-wrap slide switch, promo code, a turning card preview, a confirmation dialog sealed with a gold stamp
