          Archive · a file manager in a mid-century office

          Every surface is one physical kit of parts. The cabinet and drawer bank are quarter-sawn oak under satin varnish: a procedural grain with ray flecks, graded with the kit wood texture and lit by the scene light. Folders are manila and kraft card with alternating half-cut tabs and papers peeking out; files are ruled index cards with typed labels, inked kind glyphs and rubber-stamped status. Toolbars are green-grey enamelled steel with an orange-peel powder coat; breadcrumbs sit in pressed-steel label holders with a brass rivet each side.

          Selection is physical: a card lifts toward the light with a longer cast shadow and gets a red grease-pencil tick. Drag cards onto a folder, a drawer or a breadcrumb; the folder lifts its tab and opens its front to receive them. Turn the light dial in the bar: every shadow, bevel, rivet and varnish sheen follows it.
