          Archive · a file manager in a mid-century office

          One kit of parts: a quarter-sawn oak cabinet under satin varnish (procedural latewood lines, open pores and ray fleck), manila and kraft folders, ruled index cards, green-grey enamelled steel and brass. Labels are typed; metadata is set in a quiet sans so it stays legible at small sizes.

          Selection is physical: a card lifts toward the light and gets a grease-pencil tick. The open drawer is pulled out with a red signal tab. Drag cards onto a folder, drawer or breadcrumb. Turn the light dial: every shadow, bevel and sheen follows it.

          • Drawers (locations, drop targets), tag stickers (filters), storage tube; slide switch and brass slider in the status bar.
          • Steel keys Back / Forward, bakelite primary New folder, red enamel Discard, rocker keys Grid / List, sort slip listbox.
          • Cards: click, Shift/Ctrl-click, arrows, Enter opens, F2 renames, Del discards, Shift+F10 or long-press for the menu, Alt+← back, / search.
